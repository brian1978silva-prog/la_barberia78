## Brand & Style

The brand evokes the tactile, ritualistic discipline of classic European master-barbering paired with contemporary bespoke tailoring. Positioned in the Pyrenees high-valley setting of Andorra, the emotional tone is intimate, masculine, poised, and enduring. It appeals to discerning clientele seeking meticulous personal grooming, artisanal hot towel treatments, and traditional straight-razor craftsmanship delivered in a private, speak-easy lounge atmosphere.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Modernism** with **Vintage Editorial Refinement**. It rejects clinical brightness and flat minimalism in favor of moody chiaroscuro lighting, deep oiled-leather tones, weathered cast-iron structural weight, and refined brushed metallic highlights. The experience should feel like walking into a private heritage club: quiet luxury, substantial materials, crisp precision lines, and warm amber reflections.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an authentic dark spectrum governed by aged leather, cold-rolled steel, and burnished gold.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: Antique guilders gold used for primary callouts, signature strokes, active accents, and premium badge insignias.
- **Secondary (`#C68B59`)**: Burnished copper used for secondary highlights, hover states, and interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#3B271A`)**: Deep saddle leather brown, anchoring foundational cards, interactive hover panels, and warm ambient overlays.
- **Neutral Base (`#121214`)**: Charcoal black absolute, absorbing glare and forming the canvas backdrop.

### Supporting Functional Layers
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#1A1A1E`)**: Primary container background for cards, modals, and sheets.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#24242A`)**: Raised micro-surfaces, tooltips, and elevated interactive states.
- **Leather Midtone (`#5A3D28`)**: Accent outlines, pressed button states, and highlight borders.
- **Parchment Primary Text (`#F4EDE4`)**: Soft natural off-white preventing stark contrast fatigue while maintaining strict readability.
- **Parchment Muted Text (`#E2D9CE` / `#A3988C`)**: Secondary body copy, timestamps, captions, and de-emphasized metadata.
- **Hairline Border Gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`)**: Hairline divisions providing structural geometry without aggressive glare.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast editorial heritage with clean, geometric legibility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical pedigree, tradition, and bespoke luxury. Used for page titles, category headings, barber biographical titles, and primary service names. High-contrast serifs reflect traditional print and engraved gold-leaf glass signage.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and legibility against dark backgrounds. Used across booking calendars, pricing matrixes, time selections, and body descriptions.
- **Case Transformation**: `label-md` and `label-sm` are systematically set in uppercase with tracking between `+0.08em` and `+0.14em` to mimic luxury vintage insignias, packaging labels, and horological detailing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a structured, editorial 12-column grid system bounded by a maximum content container of `78rem` (1248px) to retain an intimate, magazine-spread atmosphere.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column symmetrical layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `5rem` page margins. Content prioritizes asymmetrical side-by-side pairings (e.g., service narrative left 5 columns, booking time matrix right 7 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Complex tables collapse into sequential, elevated card blocks.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Sticky bottom bar pattern accommodates one-handed appointment booking and service selections.

Vertical cadence relies on an 8px root grid increment. Sections breathe with generous top and bottom padding (`4.5rem` to `6rem` on desktop; `3rem` on mobile) to maintain an unhurried, luxury demeanor.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material cues rather than synthetic dropped shadows: warm amber lighting, brushed metal rim lighting, and tiered charcoal-leather strata.

- **Base Canvas**: Raw `#121214` with a very fine (1.5% opacity) organic paper/grain texture layer to break flat digital sterility.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Service Shelves)**: Solid `#1A1A1E` background surrounded by a 1px perimeter outline composed of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`. Ambient glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus)**: Deep leather blend `#24201E` backed by an active directional amber rim shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- **Spotlight Vignetting**: Key feature heroes and selected barber profiles employ subtle radial gradients in the background (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(59, 39, 26, 0.4) 0%, rgba(18, 18, 20, 0) 70%)`), imitating directional vintage overhead salon lamps.

## Shapes

The shape system is deliberate, architectural, and grounded (`roundedness: 1`). Elements favor clean, tailored edges with minimal radii to reflect traditional razor steel, straight leather strops, and bevelled glass mirrors.

- Standard buttons, text inputs, and actionable chips use a tight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, calendar surfaces, and dialog panels use an architectural `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Decorative accent tags, product quality seals, and floating time chips use an inner hairline frame inset by 2px to mimic luxury watch faces and certified apothecary labels. Circular geometries are reserved strictly for master barber avatars and artisanal stamp seals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gold Guilder)**: Solid `#D4AF37` fill with deep `#121214` text set in `label-lg` uppercase. Hover transitions smoothly to burnished copper `#C68B59` with an amber halo shadow (`0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action (Tailored Strop)**: Transparent fill with an authentic 1px border in `#5A3D28` and `#F4EDE4` text. On hover, background floods with `#3B271A` and the border illuminates to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost/Text Button**: Parchment text with a bottom 1px brass underline accent positioned 4px beneath the text baseline; shifts to gold on hover with right chevron translation.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crafted with a layered background: base `#1A1A1E` with an inner 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- Hovering initiates an elevation lift: border tint increases to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` and a faint gradient sheen sweeps from top to bottom.
- Service cards display the service title (`Playfair Display`), duration, and price in a bespoke tabular layout flanked by a dotted vintage receipt leader line.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#161619` with a recessed inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`).
- Default border: 1px solid `rgba(226, 217, 206, 0.15)`. Text in `#F4EDE4`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D4AF37` accompanied by a micro 1px amber glow; the floating label shifts upward in gold uppercase `label-sm`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Compact elements with 4px radii. Inactive states feature `#1A1A1E` background with `#A3988C` text.
- Active states feature `#3B271A` leather background, gold perimeter outline, and `#F4EDE4` text accompanied by a subtle gold dot indicator.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- Custom architectural squares with 2px radius or circles.
- Inactive: 1.5px border in `#5A3D28`, dark interior.
- Selected: Solid gold background (`#D4AF37`) with dark charcoal inner icon/dot checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Barber Availability Carousel**: Horizontal cards featuring circular barber portraits encased in a 2px copper ring, rating, specialties, and real-time status chips ("Next Available: 15:30").
- **Treatment Menu Accordion**: Expandable tiers with gold Roman numerals (I, II, III), detailing hot towel beard trims, straight-razor shaves, and scalp treatments.
- **Time Slot Matrix**: Grid of square, bevelled blocks showing available hours; selected slots exhibit a gold border and leather brown fill.
